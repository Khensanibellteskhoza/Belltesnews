Editorial policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Belltes News is an independent South African digital news publisher. We research, write, edit and publish original news articles covering matters of public interest, with particular attention to South Africa’s provinces, municipalities and communities. Our website is organised by province, municipality and subject to help readers find relevant reporting. Articles published under the Belltes News name are editorial content produced for this publication, unless a page clearly identifies material as sponsored, contributed, syndicated or otherwise sourced from another publisher.

The labels “Belltes Local Desk” and “Provincial News Desk” identify our editorial sections. Belltes News takes responsibility for the material it publishes under its name and aims to ensure that each article is researched, reviewed and presented in accordance with this policy.

Editorial purpose and responsibility

Our purpose is to provide readers with accurate, useful, independent and accessible reporting about South Africa and the wider world. We seek to explain significant developments, provide relevant context and hold ourselves accountable for the accuracy and fairness of the articles we publish. Articles carry an appropriate byline or editorial attribution where applicable.

Sources, attribution and third-party material

Our journalists and editors use identifiable and credible sources, including official records, primary documents, public statements, interviews, subject-matter experts and reputable reporting. Sources are assessed for relevance, reliability, independence and proximity to the facts. We provide attribution and links where appropriate, particularly when referring to documents, datasets, statements or reporting produced by others. We respect copyright and do not present another publisher’s protected work as our own.

Facts, quotations, names, dates, locations and statistics are checked against the best available evidence before publication. Where possible, important claims are confirmed using primary sources or more than one reliable source. Information that cannot be independently confirmed is clearly attributed and described with appropriate care rather than presented as established fact. We distinguish reporting from opinion, analysis, sponsored material and commentary.

Accuracy, fairness and right of reply

We aim to distinguish verified facts from allegations, claims, opinion, commentary and analysis. Allegations about identifiable people or organisations should not be presented as proven facts. Where a report raises material criticism or allegations, we will seek a fair and reasonable response from the affected party when appropriate and reasonably possible, and accurately describe whether a response was received.

Headlines, summaries, captions and images should not materially distort the underlying information. We do not knowingly publish fabricated quotations, invented sources or facts presented as verified when they are not. A person’s or organisation’s response should not be edited in a way that materially changes its meaning.

Corrections, updates and complaints

We take credible reports of factual errors seriously. A correction request should identify the relevant Belltes News page or item, explain the alleged error, identify the exact wording or claim in question, and include reliable supporting material or a link to the relevant primary source. We will review a substantiated request and, where a material error is confirmed in content controlled by Belltes News, correct or clarify it in a proportionate and timely way.

If a concern relates to material originally published by another organisation and merely quoted, linked or referenced by Belltes News, we will review our use of that material and correct our own article where necessary. We cannot amend content on another publisher’s website. A correction request does not automatically require removal of lawful material; we assess each request on its merits, the evidence supplied and the public interest.

Independence, advertising and sponsored material

Advertising, sponsorship, affiliate relationships and other paid promotions must be clearly distinguishable from editorial material. Payment or another commercial relationship must not be presented as independent reporting. The appearance of an advertisement does not mean Belltes News endorses the advertiser, product or service. Editorial decisions should be made independently of advertising and commercial interests.

Use of artificial intelligence and automation

Where artificial-intelligence or automated tools are used in preparing original editorial material, a human editor should review the material before publication. Such tools must not be used to invent sources, quotations, events, evidence or factual claims. Materially synthetic or altered images, audio or video should not be presented as genuine documentary evidence; where their use could mislead readers, the material should be clearly identified.

Privacy, dignity and harm

Coverage should respect privacy, dignity and applicable law. Extra care should be taken with children, victims of crime, survivors of abuse, grieving families and people in vulnerable circumstances. Identifying details should not be published merely because they are available; there should be a clear and legitimate public-interest reason, and the likely harm should be considered.

2. The information this website may process

Technical and usage information. When a page is requested, the website’s hosting, security and delivery providers may process technical records such as the Internet Protocol (IP) address, date and time of the request, requested page or URL, referring page, browser type and version, device or operating-system information, and diagnostic or security events. IP addresses and online identifiers may qualify as personal information where they identify or can reasonably be linked to an individual. The exact records and retention periods depend on the providers and services actually used.

Information you send to us. You are not required to create an account to read our publicly available articles. If you contact our newsroom, submit a correction or right-of-reply request, send a news tip, or communicate with us through a contact channel we provide, we process the contact details and message content you choose to send. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry or submission.

Advertising and third-party information. If advertising, analytics, embedded material or other third-party tools are enabled, those providers may receive technical information and identifiers through the browser when their code or content loads. The information collected depends on the service, your settings, your location and any consent choices you make.

3. Why information may be processed

Information may be processed, where lawful and necessary, to deliver and maintain the website; make pages function; protect the website and visitors against fraud, abuse and security incidents; diagnose technical problems; respond to enquiries and correction requests; comply with legal obligations; and operate, measure and improve services that the operator has actually enabled. Advertising and analytics purposes apply only when the relevant service is installed and active.

Under POPIA, the operator must have a lawful justification for processing personal information. Depending on the circumstances, this may include your consent, steps connected with a request you make, compliance with a legal duty, or another justification permitted by POPIA. Where consent is relied upon, you may withdraw it, subject to lawful consequences and processing already undertaken.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may include pixels, tags, local storage, web beacons and advertising identifiers. They may be used to remember settings, support security, measure activity, limit repeated advertisements, measure advertising performance or help deliver advertisements. We use cookies and similar technologies only for purposes connected with operating, securing, measuring or supporting the website and its services, subject to applicable law and any consent choices required.

Essential technologies may be needed for a service or security feature to work. Non-essential analytics or advertising technologies should be activated only in accordance with applicable law and the visitor’s valid choices where consent is required. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings, although blocking them may affect some website features and browser controls do not necessarily replace a legally required consent mechanism.

5. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

Belltes News may display advertising served by Google AdSense. When Google advertising services are active on the website, Google and its third-party partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store and access information on your browser. They may also use web beacons, IP addresses or other online identifiers in connection with the delivery, measurement, frequency control, personalisation and security of advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to Belltes News and/or other websites. Information handled in this context may include the page URL, IP address, browser or device information, cookie or advertising identifiers, and interactions with advertisements. The precise processing is governed by the relevant provider’s terms, settings and privacy disclosures.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and review Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can manage Google’s advertising personalisation settings at Google Ads Settings / My Ad Center. You can also learn about industry opt-out choices at YourAdChoices. Opting out of personalised advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising; you may still see contextual or non-personalised ads. Browser settings and provider-specific choices may also be available.

Other advertising vendors or networks may participate in ad serving depending on the settings selected in the advertising account and the visitor’s location. We keep advertising-vendor disclosures and consent choices aligned with the advertising providers actually enabled on the website. The providers’ own privacy notices and settings explain their processing in greater detail.

Personal information may be disclosed to hosting, security, maintenance, email, analytics or advertising providers where those providers are actually used and the disclosure is lawful and necessary for their services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents, protect rights or enforce lawful terms. Our articles may contain links to official records, source documents, research, interviews, public statements and other third-party websites. When you follow an external link, the external service may collect information directly from your browser under its own terms and privacy policy. Belltes News does not control those external services. Review their privacy notices before interacting with them.

Retention periods may differ between hosting logs, correspondence, security records and third-party advertising systems. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Subject to the conditions and limitations in applicable law, you may ask whether the operator holds personal information about you, request access to that information, request correction or deletion of inaccurate, excessive or unlawfully processed information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and exercise any other rights available under POPIA. Requests should be sent to the Privacy / Information Officer using the completed contact details in section 1.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa. The Regulator’s published contact details include POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, telephone 010 023 5200, and its official contact page. Confirm the current process and required complaint form on the Regulator’s website.

A parent, guardian or other authorised person who believes a child’s personal information has been submitted unlawfully may contact the operator using the details in section 1.
